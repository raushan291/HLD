# Manufacturing Defect Detection

A manufacturing plant has multiple conveyor belts, with one camera installed above each belt. 
Products move continuously, and the system must automatically identify defective products, 
record the results, and optionally send a reject command to the factory's PLC.

# Requirements and assumptions

Functional requirements
- Capture a continuous video stream from one camera per conveyor belt.
- Detect products moving along the conveyor.
- Inspect each product for defects (scratches, cracks, dents, missing components, etc.).
- Classify each inspected product as PASS, FAIL, or REVIEW.
- Record the defect category, confidence, product ID, camera ID, and timestamp.
- Send the inspection result to a metadata queue for storage and monitoring.
- Optionally signal the PLC to reject defective products.

Non-functional requirements
- CPU-based real-time inference
- Target processing latency of approximately 100 ms per product
- Process every product exactly once logically, even if duplicate messages occur.
- Support multiple cameras/conveyor belts.
- Handle dropped frames, RTSP disconnects, and inference failures.
- Minimize production-line disruption.
